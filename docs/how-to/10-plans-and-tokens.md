# Plans and tokens

New servers receive **1,000 Lerche Tokens** as a small starter allowance to try Lerche, using **ElevenLabs Flash v2.5** by default. Voice generation costs money, so the free allowance is limited. Lerche warns you when your balance reaches 100 tokens or fewer, then pauses TTS when your tokens run out.

Plans share benefits with everyone in the server. Check Lerche's Discord store for available products.

| Plan    | Tokens each billing month | Unused monthly tokens             |
| ------- | ------------------------: | --------------------------------- |
| Starter |                    15,000 | Expire at the end of that month   |
| Basic   |                    35,000 | Expire at the end of that month   |
| Plus    |                    70,000 | Can roll over into one more month |

Starter and Basic include standard voices. Plus also includes emotion tags, voice selection, and higher voice modules.

Billing months follow your subscription date. Administrators can check tokens with `/info-token-count`.

## How Plus rollover works

While paid access continues, unused Plus tokens expire one calendar month after their billing period ends. Tokens expiring soonest are spent first.

For example, 10,000 unused January tokens can be spent alongside February's allowance. Any January tokens left at the end of February expire.

## Top-ups

With Manage Server permission, the buyer runs `/tokens-redeem` to claim all eligible token packs for that server. Tokens cannot be moved between servers. Top-up tokens do not expire.

## Cancelling or changing plans

Cancelling keeps your plan benefits until your paid access ends. After that, the plan's features and unused monthly tokens are removed. Separately purchased top-up tokens remain.

A plan refund reverses the current billing month's tokens, including spent tokens. Earlier unspent tokens expire. A top-up refund reverses its original grant. A shortfall pauses TTS.

For purchase help, use `/help`.
